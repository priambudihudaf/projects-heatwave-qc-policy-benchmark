# Targeted 2025–2026 novelty review — research v2.1

**Purpose:** position the *retrospective heatwave-aware QC review-prioritization* pilot. This is a **targeted comparator list**, not proof of exhaustive global novelty. Details should be checked in full texts and citation metadata before publication.

The research does **not** introduce heatwave-preserving QC, budgeted human review, event-level counterfactual scoring, or downstream QC impact analysis as universally new ideas. Candidate contribution: a **controlled, matched-flag/matched-budget comparison** of label-blind event-impact review priority versus anomaly, hot-run, threshold proximity and random heuristics, with heatwave-continuity outcomes and synthetic point-error correction trade-offs.
